# 🤝 Find a Partner

> Part of [Product 2](./README.md) · **Goal: partner confirmed by Wed Nov 11**

Your **partner** is the person you're building for: someone who **runs something** and has real work that could go smoother. You're offering them something valuable: **free help from a CS student** who will listen to how they work and build a small tool or automation for them.

---

## Who makes a great partner?

✅ **Look for someone who:**
- **Runs** the business or activity, or manages a big part of it, so they can tell you how things really work
- Is **reachable**: you can visit or talk with them **twice in the next 2–3 weeks**
- Has **repeat work**: things they do every day or every week (orders, scheduling, inventory, follow-ups, records)
- Is **curious or friendly**. Enthusiasm matters more than size.

❌ **Avoid:**
- Huge companies (you'll never reach the decision-maker)
- Anyone who wants you to build a full app with payments, logins and everything by Nov 30. Your job is an **MVP**.
- Situations involving sensitive data you shouldn't touch: medical records, banking, or anything involving minors' personal information

> 👨‍👩‍👧 **Family counts.** A relative's barbershop, food truck, cleaning business, Etsy shop or tax-prep side hustle is a great partner. You already have trust, and you can visit easily.

## How to ask: in person first

**Why in person?** Small business owners are busy, get lots of spam, and decide based on trust. A friendly face in their shop beats an email every time. You also get to **see how the business works**, which is half of discovery.

### Before you go
- [ ] **Pick 2–3 places**, so you have backups.
- [ ] **Go at a slow time**: mid-morning or mid-afternoon on a weekday. Never during a lunch or dinner rush.
- [ ] **Practice your intro** out loud (below). Keep it under 30 seconds.
- [ ] **Bring a leave-behind:** a small card or half-page note with your name, "Lehman College CS student," what you're offering, and how to reach you (school email or phone).
- [ ] **Have your Lehman ID**, and **go with your teammate** if you're in a pair.

### 🗣️ In-person script (about 30 seconds)

> "Hi! I'm **[name]**, a computer science student at **Lehman College**. For a class project, I'm looking for a local business I can help **for free**. I'd like to learn how you run things day to day, and then build a small tool or automation that saves you some time, like **[example that fits them: reminders for appointments / tracking orders / a simple sign-up sheet]**.
>
> All it would take is **two short conversations, about 30 minutes each**, at a time that works for you. Is that something you'd be open to? Or is there a better time I could come back and talk?"

**If they say yes:** schedule conversation #1 right then (day and time), and get the best way to reach them.
**If they're busy:** *"No problem! Can I leave this with you and come back [day]?"* Leave your card.
**If they say no:** *"Totally understand. Thanks for your time!"* Then go to your next place. **A no is normal.** Most people need 2–3 asks.

### Answers to questions they'll ask
| They ask… | You say… |
|---|---|
| "Does it cost anything?" | "No, it's completely free. It's for my class." |
| "What do I have to do?" | "Just two short conversations: one so I can learn how things work, and one at the end so you can try what I built and tell me what you think." |
| "Will you need my passwords or customer info?" | "No. I'll only ask about how the work happens. Anything you share stays private, and I'll ask before I write down the business name." |
| "Will I have to keep using it?" | "Only if it helps! If it does, I'll show you how to use it after the semester." |
| "What can you build?" | "That depends on what would actually help you. That's why I want to listen first. It could be a simple app, or an automation that connects tools you already use." |

## Templates for after (or when you can't visit)

Use these to **follow up** after an in-person visit, or to reach family or someone you can't visit easily. Replace everything in [brackets].

### ✉️ Follow-up text or DM (after a visit)
```text
Hi [Name], it's [your name], the Lehman College student who stopped by
[business] today. Thanks for being open to helping with my class project!
Just confirming our chat on [day] at [time], about 30 min. Looking forward
to learning how you run things. 🙂
```

### ✉️ Email (when you can't visit first)
```text
Subject: Lehman College student offering free help for [Business Name]

Hi [Name],

My name is [your name], and I'm a computer science student at Lehman
College. For a class project this semester, I'm partnering with a local
business to learn how it runs and build a small, free tool or automation
that saves time on everyday work, like scheduling, orders or follow-ups.

It would take two short conversations (about 30 minutes each) between now
and [Nov 29]: one so I can understand how things work, and one so you can
try what I built and give feedback. I'd love to stop by in person if that's
easier.

Would you be open to it? I'm happy to work around your schedule.

Thank you,
[Your name]
Computer Science, Lehman College
[school email] · [phone]
```

### 👨‍👩‍👧 For a family member or friend
```text
Hey [Name]! For my coding class, I need to help a real business by
learning how it works and building something small that saves time. Could
I interview you about [the shop / your business] for about 30 min this
week? I'd want to hear what takes up the most time or what gets annoying.
Then I'll build something and show you before Thanksgiving.
```

> 🔁 **Even with family, treat it like a real client.** Schedule the conversation, take notes, and follow up. That's the skill you're practicing.
